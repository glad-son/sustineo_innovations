The Technology
How P-MFCs Work

P-MFCs tap an invisible energy cycle beneath every patch of soil. Plants feed soil bacteria; bacteria release electrons; we capture those electrons as electricity.

01
🌱

Plant Photosynthesis

Plants convert CO₂ and sunlight into organic sugars. Up to 70% of these compounds are released into the soil through roots as exudates.

02
🦠

Microbial Oxidation

Electroactive bacteria in the rhizosphere break down root exudates, releasing electrons and protons in the process.

03
⚡

Electron Capture

An anode buried near the roots captures free electrons. They flow through an external circuit — generating continuous electricity.

04
🔋

Usable Power

Collected electricity is conditioned and stored or used directly to power sensors, lights, and devices in remote areas.

About Us
Innovations Rooted in Nature

Sustineo Innovations Private Limited is a green-tech startup based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, focused on developing and deploying Plant Microbial Fuel Cell (P-MFC) technology as a scalable, sustainable energy solution for India.

The name "Sustineo" — Latin for "to sustain" — reflects our core mission: building energy systems that work with nature, not against it. We believe the future of energy is biological, decentralised, and accessible.

Our R&D combines soil microbiology, electrochemistry, and field-deployable engineering to create systems that generate electricity continuously from living plants — rice paddies, wetlands, grasslands, and more.
